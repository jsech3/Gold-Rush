GOLD RUSH — Daily Cheat Sheet

jobhunt.daitiq.com · pw: goldrush

What This Is

Gold Rush is an automated job hunting system that runs twice a day (8am + 7pm). It pulls jobs from 3 sources, scores them 0–100, and ranks them into tiers. You see the results on a private dashboard — no more digging through email alerts or scrolling LinkedIn.

SourceWhat It Pulls
JSearch APIFresh postings from job boards (Indeed, ZipRecruiter, etc.)
LinkedIn AlertsYour email job alerts, parsed automatically
Hacker NewsMonthly "Who's Hiring" threads, filtered to your roles

Job Tiers

TierScoreAction
GOLD 70–84 Apply immediately. Strong match on title, salary, freshness.
SILVER 50–69 Apply if interesting. Partial match — maybe off on location or pay.
BRONZE <50 Skip. Weak match. Only look if nothing else is available.

Daily Routine

Morning Check (after 8am)

  • Open jobhunt.daitiq.com
  • Filter to GOLD tier
  • Open each Gold job, read it, apply if it fits
  • Check the applied box after submitting
  • ✕ dismiss anything fake, irrelevant, or expired
  • Scan SILVER for standout companies

Evening Check (after 7pm)

  • Set date filter to "today"
  • Review any new Gold/Silver jobs from the afternoon
  • Apply + check boxes as needed

Weekly Cleanup (Fridays)

  • Open the Tracker (click "Applied: N →")
  • Review stale jobs (yellow, 14d+) — follow up or let go
  • Archive cold jobs (red, 21d+) — click "Archive all cold"
  • Check Dismissed page to see patterns (too many fakes? wrong sources?)

Application Aging

Your tracked applications automatically change color over time:

StatusDaysWhat to Do
ACTIVE 0–13 Sit tight. Normal response window.
STALE 14–20 Probably ghosted. Send a follow-up or move on.
COLD 21+ Dead. Archive it to keep your tracker clean.
The Goal

Apply to every Gold job that's a real match. Aim for 2–5 applications per day. By the end of each week, you should have 10–20+ fresh applications out, with stale ones cleared and your tracker reflecting only live opportunities.
