GOLD RUSH DASHBOARD

Usage guide // jobhunt.daitiq.com

How It Works

Gold Rush runs automatically twice a day (8am and 7pm). It scrapes jobs from JSearch API, LinkedIn email alerts, and Hacker News, then scores each job 0–100 and classifies it into a tier. After each run, the dashboard at jobhunt.daitiq.com updates automatically with the latest results.

Daily Workflow

Morning (after 8am run)

  1. Open jobhunt.daitiq.com on your phone or laptop
  2. Filter to Gold tier — these are your best matches
  3. Click Apply on any Gold jobs that look good — opens the posting in a new tab
  4. Check the applied checkbox after you submit so you don't revisit them
  5. Glance at Silver if you have time — decent but less aligned

Evening (after 7pm run)

  1. Same routine — check for new jobs that came in during the day
  2. Use the "today" date filter to see only fresh ones

What the Tiers Mean

Tier Score What to Do
Gold 70–84 Apply immediately — strong title + salary + freshness match
Silver 50–69 Apply if interesting — partial match, maybe wrong location or lower salary
Bronze <50 Skip unless something catches your eye

Filters & Controls

Recommended filters
  • Tier buttons — Click Gold to show only top-tier jobs
  • "today" date range — Skip stale listings, focus on fresh ones
  • "Hide applied" checkbox — Toggle on to avoid re-reviewing jobs
  • Search box — Type a company name or keyword to narrow results
  • Sort — Default is by score; switch to "date" to see newest first

Application Tracker

Every time you check the applied checkbox on a job, the date you marked it is recorded. You can review all your applications on the Tracker page.

How to use
  • Click "Applied: N →" in the stats bar to open the Tracker
  • Applications are grouped by date, newest first
  • Click the ✕ on any entry to un-mark it as applied
  • Click "← Dashboard" to go back

Application Aging

Applications automatically change color based on how long ago you applied. This keeps your tracker clean and honest — no dead opportunities cluttering the view.

Status Days Visual What It Means
Active 0–13 Green border Still in play — waiting to hear back
Stale 14–20 Yellow border + day badge Probably ghosted — follow up or let go
Cold 21+ Red border + day badge Dead — moved to Cold section at bottom
Keeping it clean
  • Cold jobs (21d+) appear in a separate "Cold — No Response" section at the bottom of the tracker
  • Each cold job has an Archive button to dismiss it individually
  • Use the "Archive all cold jobs" button to bulk-clear everything 21+ days old in one tap
  • Archived cold jobs go to the Dismissed page under the "No Response" category

Dismissing Jobs

If a job is irrelevant, fake, or no longer available, you can dismiss it to remove it from the main dashboard.

How to dismiss
  1. Click the ✕ button on any job row
  2. A modal asks why you are removing it:
ReasonWhen to use
No longer availableJob listing has been taken down or filled
Fake jobScam posting, fake company, or recruiter bait
Not relevantWrong role, wrong industry, or bad title match
Just didn't likeSomething about it turned you off
OtherCustom reason — type your own note

After selecting a reason, click Remove to confirm. The job disappears from the dashboard.

Dismissed Jobs Page

All dismissed jobs are tracked and reviewable.

How to review
  • Click "Dismissed: N →" in the stats bar (appears after you dismiss at least one job)
  • Jobs are grouped by reason (Fake Job, No Longer Available, Not Relevant, etc.)
  • Each entry shows the job details, dismiss date, and any custom note
  • Click the ↩ button to restore a job back to the main dashboard

Data Storage

Important
  • Applied status, dismiss reasons, and dates are all saved in your browser's localStorage
  • Use the same device and browser consistently to keep your data intact
  • Clearing browser data will erase your applied/dismissed history
  • Job data (titles, scores, companies) comes from jobs.json which updates automatically with each Gold Rush run

Tips

  • The dashboard refreshes automatically with each Gold Rush run — no action needed from you
  • On mobile, tap the job title itself to open the posting (the Apply button is hidden on small screens)
  • The stats bar at the top shows how many Gold/Silver/Bronze jobs are in the system and when the last run happened
  • Source labels: JSearch = API scrape, LinkedIn = email alerts, HN = Hacker News
